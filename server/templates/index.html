How the Automation Pipeline Works

8 coordinated stages ensuring precision and text preservation

1. Reading & Extraction

Extracts all paragraphs, in-text citation markers [N], figures, tables, and raw reference entries. Generates an untouched backup copy.

2. Isolated Zotero Sync

If an account or temporary Zotero key is present, securely fetches library metadata in-memory. Cross-user isolation guarantees private keys remain 100% confidential.

3. Online Verification

Validates paper titles against Crossref and Semantic Scholar, automatically retrieving canonical DOIs, official journal abbreviations, and page ranges.

4. Chronological Mapping

Scans document text in sequential reading order and reassigns [1], [2], [3] per strict IEEE first-appearance regulations.

5. Ctrl+Click Hyperlinks

Embeds native Word bookmarks and hyperlink anchors. Readers can Ctrl+Click any marker in Word to immediately jump to the bibliography entry, with hover ScreenTips.

6. Mathematical Text Audit

Diffs every single paragraph word-for-word. Mathematical validation guarantees that zero prose or sentence wording was altered outside citation brackets.
